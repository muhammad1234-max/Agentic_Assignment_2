GOVERNMENT OF SINDH - HEALTH DEPARTMENT
DIRECTORATE GENERAL HEALTH SERVICES, SINDH (HYDERABAD / KARACHI)
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOPS) FOR PREVENTION AND CLINICAL MANAGEMENT OF HEATSTROKE

1. Public Healthcare Directives for Karachi Metropolitan Division:
All tertiary care and district headquarters hospitals (Civil Hospital Karachi, JPMC, Abbasi Shaheed Hospital) and basic health units are placed on high alert during designated heatwave alerts.
- Dedicated Heatstroke Stabilization Centers (HSCs) are established with air-conditioned resuscitation bays, ice packs, intravenous cold normal saline, and oral rehydration stations.

2. School and Child Safety Measures:
- Schools must suspend outdoor physical education, sports days, and morning assemblies during heat alerts.
- Classrooms must ensure adequate cross-ventilation, functional fans, and readily accessible clean drinking water with ORS.
- School dismissal timings must be shifted forward to 11:30 AM or 12:00 PM to prevent children from traveling during peak noon heat (12:00 PM to 02:00 PM).
- Parents and school vans must ensure children wear breathable light cotton uniforms and carry sun protection.

3. Occupational Safety for Outdoor Laborers:
- Employers and contractors must provide shaded rest shelters and mandatory 15-minute rest breaks every hour during heavy exertion in temperatures above 38°C.
- Clean drinking water fortified with minerals/electrolytes must be made available on construction and industrial sites.
- Heavy manual labor should be scheduled during cooler early morning (06:00 AM - 10:00 AM) or late evening hours.

4. High-Risk Clinical Groups and Medications:
- Elderly patients taking anticholinergic medications, beta-blockers, antihistamines, or diuretics are impaired in sweating and blood pressure regulation.
- Patients experiencing reduced urine output, dry mouth, disorientation, or syncope must be transferred immediately to emergency facilities.
