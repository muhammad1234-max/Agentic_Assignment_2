NATIONAL DISASTER MANAGEMENT AUTHORITY (NDMA) PAKISTAN
PUBLIC ADVISORY: HEATWAVE PREPAREDNESS AND PUBLIC SAFETY GUIDELINES

Target Areas: Urban Sindh, Karachi Metropolitan Region, and Southern Plain Districts.

1. Vulnerable Populations:
Extreme heat poses severe health hazards, especially to children, infants, elderly individuals over 65 years, pregnant women, and patients suffering from pre-existing chronic conditions including cardiovascular disease, asthma, and diabetes. Outdoor laborers, construction workers, traffic police, and daily-wage earners face elevated occupational heat risk.

2. Symptoms of Heat-Related Illnesses:
- Heat Exhaustion: Heavy sweating, paleness, muscle cramps, tiredness, weakness, dizziness, headache, nausea or vomiting, and fainting. The skin may be cool and moist.
- Heatstroke (Life-Threatening Emergency): High body temperature (above 40°C / 104°F), red, hot, dry skin (absence of sweating), rapid and strong pulse, throbbing headache, confusion, slurred speech, delirium, convulsions, and unconsciousness.

3. Emergency First Aid Actions:
- Immediately move the affected individual to a shaded, well-ventilated, or air-conditioned area.
- Loosen or remove tight outer clothing.
- Apply cool wet cloths or cold packs to the neck, armpits, and groin.
- Fan the patient actively and sponge the body with cool (not ice cold) water.
- If conscious, offer cool water or oral rehydration salts (ORS) in small sips. Do not force fluids if vomiting or semi-conscious.
- Call emergency rescue services (1122 or Edhi 115 / Chhipa 1020) immediately if heatstroke is suspected.

4. Preventative Measures for Citizens:
- Avoid unnecessary outdoor exposure between 11:00 AM and 04:00 PM during peak solar radiation.
- Drink plenty of water and fluids regularly, even before feeling thirsty. Avoid carbonated, caffeinated, and alcoholic drinks.
- Wear lightweight, loose-fitting, light-colored cotton clothing. Cover your head with a cap, umbrella, or wet cloth when outdoors.
- Never leave children, disabled individuals, or pets in a parked vehicle, even for a few minutes.
